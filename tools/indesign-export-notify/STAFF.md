# Get a pop-up when an InDesign export is done

Takes 30 seconds. Use **Chrome** or **Edge**.

1. **Open the link** you were sent (it looks like `https://ntfy.sh/indesign-exports-...`).
2. Click **Allow** when the browser asks to show notifications.
3. Click **Install** (the small screen-with-arrow icon at the right of the address bar, or **⋯ → Apps → Install**).

Done. You'll get a pop-up like **✅ InDesign export finished — Done (2): Poster_A1.pdf, Banner.pdf**, even with the window closed. Open the app any time to see recent exports.

**Good to know**
- Keep your browser running in the background. That's normal for Chrome and Edge.
- If you haven't opened the app for a week, you'll get a "Notifications will be paused" message. Open the app once and it continues.
- On your phone? Install the free **ntfy** app (App Store / Google Play), tap **+**, and paste the channel name (the part after `ntfy.sh/`).
- Keep the link inside the agency. Anyone who has it can see the notifications.
